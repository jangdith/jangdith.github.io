Increased Doctor Consultations by 90% by Designing an Insurance Flow in Collaboration with the Business Team

KlikProteksi offers users the convenience of buying new insurance policies or connecting their existing ones, ensuring seamless access to various coverage options tailored to diverse needs.

Understanding the Background

KlikDokter is an Indonesian digital healthcare platform, offering 24/7 teleconsultation, hospital/medical appointment booking, health tools, medical info, and online health product purchases. It aims to provide accessible and trusted healthcare services.
The 'Tanya Dokter' feature, which allows users to consult with doctors, has received overwhelmingly positive feedback from users on various platforms. However, many users have expressed a desire for enhanced payment options, specifically the ability to use their insurance for consultations or to purchase insurance directly through the platform.

Problem

The current payment system does not accommodate insurance options, which limits the accessibility of medical consultations for users who have personal or corporate insurance. This gap in service not only affects user satisfaction but also restricts KlikDokter's growth potential in a competitive market where users increasingly expect integrated healthcare solutions.

Proposed Solutions

Enables users to pay for consultations using their insurance, requiring partnerships with various insurance providers for seamless transactions. Design an intuitive interface to guide users in selecting their insurance provider, entering policy details, and confirming payment.
Additionally, implement a feature for direct insurance purchases through KlikDokter, including tailored plans from insurance companies, and provide a comparison tool to help users evaluate different insurance options based on coverage, premiums, and benefits.
Leading the Way: Founding Designer Magic
In this project, I led the entire design process from concept to execution. As the sole Product Designer in this project, I took full ownership, from competitor analysis to high-fidelity prototypes, delivering a scalable, user-friendly design.

Impact

Implementing the insurance payment and purchase integration will significantly enhance KlikDokter by:
  • Increasing Accessibility: Users will find it easier to access healthcare services through insurance payment options and direct insurance purchases.
  • Boosting User Satisfaction: A streamlined process will lead to higher user satisfaction, as users can conveniently manage their insurance needs within the platform.
  • Driving Growth: The new features are likely to attract more users, leading to increased doctor consultations and overall platform growth.
